# OfficeTrackr

OfficeTrackr helps hybrid teams coordinate office attendance and time off.

## Language

**Office day**:
A day on which a coworker plans to work from the office.

**Vacation day**:
A day a coworker takes off from work.

**Available workday**:
A weekday that is not recorded as a vacation day for that coworker.

**Telework day**:
An available workday that a coworker has not marked as an office day.

**Attendance target**:
The monthly office-day goal, set at 40% of available workdays and rounded to a whole day.
